# Support

## Choose the right channel

- **Usage and configuration:** use the
  [support question form](https://github.com/eman/tariffkit/issues/new?template=support_question.yml).
- **Reproducible defect:** use the
  [bug report form](https://github.com/eman/tariffkit/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Incorrect or outdated published rate data:** use the
  [rate-data form](https://github.com/eman/tariffkit/issues/new?template=rate_data.yml).
- **Feature proposal:** use the
  [feature request form](https://github.com/eman/tariffkit/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Suspected vulnerability:** follow [Security](SECURITY.md) and use
  [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/eman/tariffkit/security/advisories/new).

Search existing documentation and issues before opening a new thread.
TariffKit is maintained on a best-effort basis; response times are not
guaranteed.

## Keep reports public-safe

Do not post credentials, tokens, cookies, `.env` contents, bills or statement
PDFs, account or meter identifiers, Home Assistant diagnostics, Green Button
exports, or raw utility portal responses. Reduce the problem to synthetic data
and redact identifiers rather than masking only part of them.

If private data has already been posted, rotate affected credentials and contact
the maintainer through their GitHub profile to request removal. Do not quote or
repost the exposed material.

## What support can cover

Support can help explain documented behavior, reproduce TariffKit defects, and
identify which published source produced vendored rate data. It cannot verify a
utility bill, interpret an individual service agreement, provide legal or
financial advice, or troubleshoot private utility-account access in public.
